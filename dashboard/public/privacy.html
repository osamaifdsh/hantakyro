Hantakyro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hantakyro ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot collects and processes the minimum information required to provide its protection and moderation services. This may include:

  • Discord IDs: Server (guild) IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs, used to apply protection rules and configuration.
  • Server configuration: Protection module settings, whitelists, log channel settings, welcome/goodbye messages, and auto-role settings, as chosen by server administrators.
  • Action logs: Records of moderation events (e.g., anti-ban, anti-spam, punishments) including the affected user IDs and the reason, stored temporarily to provide the log feature.
  • Message metadata: Only in real time, to detect spam, links, or mass mentions. We do not permanently store message content.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect passwords, email addresses, or payment information.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or tracking.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Bot's protection and moderation features.
  • To detect and prevent spam, raids, links, and other abuse.
  • To improve the Bot's reliability and performance.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Configuration data is stored in files managed by the Bot and is retained only while the Bot operates.
  • Action logs are limited (the most recent records are kept) and are used only for the log feature.
  • If the Bot is removed from your server, its configuration for that server will no longer be applied. You may request deletion of stored data at any time (see Section 8).

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through the Discord API. Your interaction with the Bot is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. We do not control how Discord processes data on its platform.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data related to your server or account at any time by contacting us. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
